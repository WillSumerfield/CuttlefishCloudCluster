title: Donating
---
To donate a machine, you lend its GPUs to the cluster as an [[Glossary#Arm|Arm]]. Other people's Dives
run on it while you're not using it, and you earn [[Glossary#Karma|Karma]] for that. These pages are for
[[Glossary#Donor|Donors]]: how to set an Arm up, what it does day to day, and the settings you control.

## Pages
- [[Arm setup]]: turn your machine into an Arm, update it, or remove it
- [[Arms]]: what runs on your Arm, what you earn, and how to take it back
- [[Arm features]]: tell CCC about special software on your machine
